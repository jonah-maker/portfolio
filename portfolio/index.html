AW25 Collection

Noir Essence

Concept

Noir Essence is a meditation on darkness — not as absence, but as a presence. This collection explores the weight and warmth of black, revealing the spectrum of emotion hidden within a single color. Each piece is an architecture of shadow, constructed to move with the body like a second skin.

Design Inspiration

Inspired by the charcoal drawings of Robert Longo, the brutalist architecture of Tadao Ando, and the quiet power of Japanese ink painting. The collection channels the tension between strength and vulnerability, between armor and intimacy.

Fabrics

Japanese wool crêpe, double-faced cashmere, silk organza, bonded neoprene, hand-waxed cotton

Techniques

Laser-cut paneling, ultrasonic seam bonding, hand-pressed pleating, raw-edge finishing

Palette

Jet black, charcoal, obsidian, smoke grey, off-black

Creative Process

The collection began with a series of draping experiments on the stand, working exclusively in black muslin. Each garment was deconstructed and rebuilt multiple times, stripping away the unnecessary until only the essential remained. The final pieces carry the memory of their making — raw edges, visible construction, and the ghost of the hand.

SS25 Collection

Desert Mirage

Concept

Desert Mirage captures the ephemeral beauty of heat shimmer and sand dunes — garments that appear to shift and dissolve as they move. This collection is an ode to the nomadic spirit, where clothing becomes shelter, landscape, and identity all at once.

Design Inspiration

Drawn from the textiles of the Saharan Tuareg, the land art of Walter De Maria, and the photography of Nadav Kander. The palette is born from the earth itself — sun-bleached bone, terracotta at dusk, and the silver-blue of desert nights.

Fabrics

Washed linen, raw silk tussah, hand-loomed cotton, vegetable-tanned leather, hemp-silk blend

Techniques

Natural dye baths (indigo, walnut, pomegranate), hand-smocking, sand-washed finishing, macramé knotwork

Palette

Sand, bone white, terracotta, dusty rose, clay, desert sage

Creative Process

Development began with a research trip to Morocco, studying the geometry of Berber textiles and the way light transforms the landscape hour by hour. Fabrics were left outdoors for weeks, exposed to sun and wind, allowing nature to collaborate in the finishing. Every garment carries the story of that exposure — the softness of time, the warmth of earth.

AW24 Collection

Urban Silhouette

Concept

Urban Silhouette is a study of the human form against the city skyline — sharp, geometric, monumental. This collection transforms the language of architecture into wearable sculpture, with clean lines that echo glass facades and structural engineering.

Design Inspiration

Inspired by the architectural photography of Hélène Binet, the geometric paintings of Kazimir Malevich, and the structural ambition of Zaha Hadid. The collection asks: what happens when a building becomes a garment?

Fabrics

Technical gabardine, bonded wool, coated cotton, laser-cut neoprene, reflective nylon

Techniques

3D pattern engineering, heat-bonded seams, architectural draping, injection-molded hardware

Palette

Concrete grey, steel blue, midnight, chalk white, graphite

Creative Process

Each silhouette began as a paper model — folded, cut, and assembled like a miniature building. These maquettes were then translated into toiles, preserving the sharp folds and cantilever structures of the original paper forms. The collection celebrates precision and the beauty of things that are engineered rather than merely sewn.

The Designer

About Jonah

Fashion is architecture for the body — a dialogue between structure and sensation, between the garment and the person who inhabits it.

Jonah is a contemporary fashion designer whose work exists at the intersection of minimalism and emotion. Trained at Central Saint Martins and mentored in the ateliers of Paris, his approach to design is rooted in the belief that every seam tells a story and every silhouette is a statement of intent.

His collections are defined by their sculptural quality — garments that appear simple on the surface but reveal extraordinary complexity in their construction. Working primarily with natural fabrics sourced from artisan mills in Japan and Italy, Jonah creates pieces that age beautifully, developing character and depth with wear.

His design philosophy rejects the disposable. Each collection is conceived as a permanent wardrobe — timeless pieces that transcend seasons and trends, built to be worn, loved, and passed on.

Behind the Scenes

The Studio

01

Research & Concept

Every collection begins with immersion — months of research into art, architecture, material science, and cultural history. Mood boards give way to material experiments, and slowly a visual language emerges.

02

Draping & Toile

Design happens on the stand, not the sketch pad. Jonah drapes directly in muslin, letting the fabric guide the form. Each toile is fitted, deconstructed, and rebuilt — sometimes dozens of times before a shape feels right.

03

Fabric Sourcing

Relationships with multigenerational mills in Biella, Kyoto, and Mumbai provide access to extraordinary textiles. Fabrics are chosen not just for how they look, but for how they drape, age, and feel against the skin.

04

Construction

Final garments are constructed in the studio by a small team of master tailors. Every seam is considered, every hem weighted, every button hand-sewn. The construction is the design — there is no separation.
